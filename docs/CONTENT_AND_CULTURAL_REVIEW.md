# Content and Cultural Review

## 1. World premise

Provisional fiction: the player becomes counter keeper of Moonwake Sushi, an
eight-seat shop on fictional Shiokaze Row. Returning guests give the world
continuity through small acts of service and neighborhood care.

These names are creative working material, not culturally approved production
copy.

## 2. Voice

Warm, observant, concise. Favor sensory specifics—cedar after rain, kettle hiss,
vinegar warmth, a curtain lifting in harbor wind. Avoid warrior catchphrases,
“ancient secret,” mash-up honorifics, decorative pseudo-Japanese, and generic
mysticism.

Japanese culinary terms appear only when accurate and useful, with plain-English
gloss on first use. Food fiction MUST NOT present unsafe real-world food-handling
instruction as authoritative guidance.

## 3. Content progression

Potential chapters after MVP:

1. **Light the Counter** — restore shelf, stools, and menu board.
2. **The Regulars’ Table** — preferences and six core recipes.
3. **A Full Noren** — choose a specialty menu and host a neighborhood evening.

Quests use setup → service constraint → guest consequence → keepsake, never
repeat-X-times grind. Events remain replayable in an archive with no streak
penalty, artificial scarcity, or fear-of-missing-out reward.

## 4. Review checklist

Named Japanese cultural and culinary reviewers must cover:

- product and shop names;
- culinary terminology, recipe construction, serving ware, and dietary tags;
- signage, Japanese-language text, seals, clothing, and architecture;
- guest naming and characterization;
- seasonal references, ceremonies, and neighborhood fiction;
- pixel art silhouettes, poses, tools, ingredients, and plating;
- sound effects, music instrumentation, voice, and ambient scenes;
- marketing copy and community event concepts.

The review record names reviewer, scope, date, findings, changes, unresolved
concerns, and approver. Unreviewed culturally specific art/text/audio blocks a
public release.

## 5. Prohibited shorthand

No random kanji, faux calligraphy, rising-sun patterns, katana UI, helmets,
geisha imagery, sacred/religious objects as loot, honor meter, warrior ranks,
faux-feudal plot, or “samurai chef” caricature. Do not force Dos Esposas lime,
jalapeño, tequila, or other inventory into sushi to manufacture compatibility.

## 6. Content delivery targets

- MVP: three dishes, three guests, one room, three restoration choices.
- Alpha: six dishes, four to eight guests, five authored quests, three chapters,
  and a reviewable asset/content pipeline.
- Post-alpha: 12–24 dishes, seasonal packs, shop variants, and crossover specials
  only after provenance and contract approval.
