# Delivery Plan

## Phase 0 — Foundation

Deliver workspace, ADRs, the finite species → ingredient → prepared component
→ dish → recipe/content-pack graph, family/variant validator, database
migrations, CI, environment contract, threat model, and walletless pixel shell.

Exit: repository gates run from a clean checkout; product and cultural open
decisions are visible; no chain/deployment claim is implied.

Before Phase 1 locks production direction, complete a named Japanese cultural
and culinary concept review of title, setting, guests, recipes, signage,
silhouettes, clothing, and audio direction. Record required changes. Final
asset/copy/audio review remains a separate release gate.

Phase 0 also decides the Phase 1 persistence/account ADR. Postgres/server
authority is a working architecture assumption, not adopted product truth,
until privacy, offline behavior, deletion, and guest-to-wallet migration are
resolved.

## Phase 1 — Evening Service prototype

Build one counter, three guests, the three-dish first service, immediate salmon
sashimi unlock, core stations, service state, feedback, pause/recovery,
save/resume, restoration choice, responsive layout, and accessibility.

Exit: the defined validation cohort finishes without a wallet; median first
serve <3 minutes; ≥80% finish; ≥60% begin a second shift within 24 hours. Phase
1 is a walletless prototype milestone, not the promised MVP release.

## Phase 2 — Shadownet keepsake

Add optional wallet link, exact review, non-financial receipt contract, chain
worker, projection, durable lifecycle, deployment manifest, and reconciliation.

Exit: exact-manifest Shadownet journey survives reload, reject, wrong network,
account change, indexer lag, RPC outage, duplicate events, and reorg simulation.
Phase 2 completes the specified MVP: Evening Service plus optional post-`SETTLED`
Shadownet keepsake.

## Phase 3 — Alpha content

Expand to 12 authored dishes across sashimi, nigiri, gunkan, hosomaki,
futomaki, and uramaki; add more guests, cookbook/service history, restoration
and memento gallery, content validation, and final cultural/culinary asset
review. Public-launch readiness separately requires 24 reviewed dishes,
including temaki, at least eight seafood identities, three roe identities, four
roll structures, and meaningful cooked/plant-forward alternatives.

Exit: content/a11y/performance gates pass; product health remains within target
after content breadth increases.

## Phase 4 — Narrow read-only interoperability

Research and, only if justified, recognize one fully verified Dos Esposas base
ingredient or DER holding for cosmetic/lore effect. No custody or consumption.

Exit: independent registry/manifest review, negative lookalike/drift fixtures,
privacy and support review, and player evidence that the feature adds meaning.

## Phase 5 — Production decision

Decide whether mainnet, transferability, foreign-asset custody, onchain dishes,
or monetization should exist. Each is a separate go/no-go backed by security,
legal/claim, economic, operational, and cultural evidence.

## Workstreams

- Product/gameplay: loop, feedback, research, metrics.
- Design/content: pixel system, accessibility, cultural review, authored data.
- Platform: API, persistence, worker, observability, privacy.
- Wallet/chain: final-dispatch guard, manifests, contract, confirmation/reorg.
- Trust: security review, hostile fixtures, admin/incident ceremonies, claims.

## Evidence discipline

Report separately:

1. source/tests/builds;
2. deployed runtime and exact manifest;
3. authenticated wallet journey;
4. mainnet readiness;
5. cultural review;
6. physical-device and accessibility evidence.

None substitutes for another.
