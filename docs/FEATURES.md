# Feature Catalog

## P0 — MVP Evening Service

| Feature | Player value | Acceptance summary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guest-first entry | play begins without crypto knowledge | start shift in one action; no wallet wall |
| Guided rice preparation | learn the shared base rhythm | forgiving tutorial; keyboard/touch/pointer |
| Three authored orders | complete service arc | kappa, tamago, salmon nigiri; clear tickets and recovery |
| First sashimi unlock | establish rice-free fish preparation | salmon sashimi unlocks after first `SETTLED` service; reviewed slice/plate path |
| Counter stations | tactile craft decisions | no drag-only or sub-5-second essential timing |
| Plating choice | expression without pay advantage | cosmetic guest feedback only |
| Service ledger | trustworthy completion and learning | accuracy/pacing/hospitality separated; durable resume |
| Restoration choice | immediate shop ownership | three cosmetic choices; no transferable value |
| Accessibility controls | functional inclusion | WCAG 2.2 AA, reduced motion, sound/shake toggles |
| Optional wallet link | provenance after earned meaning | secondary after service; exact network/account truth |
| Shadownet service receipt | inspectable keepsake | deterministic, non-financial, exactly once, confirmed only |
| Recovery lifecycle | confidence through failure | reject/cancel/outage/reload/reorg states preserve play |

## P1 — Alpha, after MVP gates

- A 12-dish authored alpha spanning sashimi, nigiri, gunkan, hosomaki,
  futomaki, and uramaki; a separately gated 24-dish public-launch catalog adds
  temaki, deeper fish/cut breadth, and three distinct roe products.
- Filterable cookbook by family, species, cut, dietary/allergen, season, and
  mastery state.
- Four to eight recurring guests and chapter flags.
- Persistent service history and memento gallery.
- Five authored neighborhood quests and three chapter arcs.
- Additional station and presentation options.
- Content validation and cultural-review records.
- Read-only recognition spike for one exact Dos Esposas asset identity.

## P2 — Research only

- Onchain cosmetic editions.
- Escrow/redeem adapter for one allowlisted same-chain asset.
- Additional shops or counter layouts.
- Archived seasonal content packs.
- Mainnet receipt deployment.

Each P2 item requires its own value, security, support, legal/claim, and
operational gate. Inclusion here is not a roadmap commitment.

## Not planned without new product direction

Marketplace, P2P trading, paid loot, staking, yield, bonding curves, referral
emissions, public wealth rankings, PvP, guilds, land, token-gated story, daily
streaks, broad bridges, automatic foreign-token consumption, or arbitrary FA2
imports.
